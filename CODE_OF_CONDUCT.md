# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume good faith. Critique the code, not the person.

## Our pledge

Everyone who takes part in this project — contributors, maintainers, people
opening issues — is entitled to a harassment-free experience, regardless of age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## What that looks like in practice

Behaviour that helps:

- Giving and accepting review feedback on the merits of the change
- Being explicit about what you tried and what you know, especially when asking
- Recognising that "obvious" depends entirely on what someone has seen before
- Taking responsibility, apologising, and moving on when you get it wrong

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit
messages, code comments — and whenever someone is representing the project in
public.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour to
**mojtaba.python@gmail.com**. Reports are handled privately and reviewed as
promptly as I can manage. The reporter's identity is not disclosed.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, code and issues that violate
this policy, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour they
consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful — with a reason
given.
